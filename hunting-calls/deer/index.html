Silent gaps of 30–75 seconds sit between sequences so you can watch and listen. Mastered loud for a phone speaker: peaks under −1 dBFS.

How to use it

  • Best from the pre-rut through peak rut, late October to late November, at first and last light.
  • On iPhone, turn the volume all the way up and turn off Settings › Sounds & Haptics › Headphone Safety › Reduce Loud Audio. A Bluetooth speaker carries much farther.
  • Bucks often circle downwind of a call. Face the speaker upwind of you and watch your downwind side.
  • Rattling works best where mature bucks are present and the buck-to-doe ratio is balanced.

Check your local regulations first. Some states ban electronic calls for deer.
