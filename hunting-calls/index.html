Hunting Calls

Each video is one photo with a 10-minute calling track as its sound, at the recordings' original volume. Save it to your iPhone photo album and play it from Photos.

Save to your photo album (iPhone)

  1. Tap Save video to Photos under a video and wait a few seconds while it loads.
  2. The share sheet opens. Tap Save Video. It goes straight to your camera roll.
  3. If the share sheet doesn't open, open the page in Safari and try again.

Tips

  • Female calls (cow, doe) bring males in looking for a mate. Male calls (bull grunts, bugles, buck fights) bring in males that want to fight.
  • Turn the volume all the way up and turn off Settings › Sounds & Haptics › Headphone Safety › Reduce Loud Audio. A Bluetooth speaker carries much farther.
  • After a track ends, stay still for 20–30 minutes. Animals often come in slow and quiet, and circle downwind.
  • Rut timing: moose mid-September to early October, elk September, whitetail late October to late November.

Check your local regulations first. Many states and provinces ban electronic calls for big game.
