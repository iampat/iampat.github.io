Hunting Calls

Each video is one photo with a 10-minute calling track as its sound. Save it to your iPhone photo album and play it from Photos.

Save to your photo album (iPhone)

  1. Open this page in Safari and tap Save to Photos under a video.
  2. In the share sheet, tap Save Video. It appears in Photos.
  3. If no share sheet opens, the video downloads instead. Open it from the Files app › Downloads, tap Share › Save Video.

Tips

  • Female calls (cow, doe) bring males in looking for a mate. Male calls (bugles, buck fights) bring in males that want to fight.
  • Turn the volume all the way up and turn off Settings › Sounds & Haptics › Headphone Safety › Reduce Loud Audio. A Bluetooth speaker carries much farther.
  • After a track ends, stay still for 20–30 minutes. Animals often come in slow and quiet, and circle downwind.
  • Rut timing: moose mid-September to early October, elk September, whitetail late October to late November.

Check your local regulations first. Many states and provinces ban electronic calls for big game.
