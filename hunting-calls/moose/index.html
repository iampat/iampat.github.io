Cow Moose Call

Download MP3 Download video (MP4)

What's on the track

  • A real cow moose estrus moan, recorded by the U.S. Fish & Wildlife Service in Minnesota.
  • Calling bouts of one or two moans, with 30–60 seconds of silence between them to listen for a bull.
  • Mastered loud for a phone speaker: about −11 LUFS, peaks under −1 dBFS.

How to use it

  • Best in the rut, mid-September to early October, at dawn and dusk.
  • On iPhone, turn the volume all the way up and turn off Settings › Sounds & Haptics › Headphone Safety › Reduce Loud Audio. A Bluetooth speaker carries much farther.
  • After the track ends, wait quietly 20–30 minutes. Bulls often come in slow and silent.

Check your local regulations first. Many states and provinces ban electronic calls for big game, including moose.
